# Zoned combat considerations



## Actions

### Dash and Speed

If your speed is 25 ft to 45 ft, then you need to take the Dash action to change zones.

If your speed is 50 ft or higher, you can use a normal move between zones, and then still use an action, e.g. attack.

If your speed is 20 ft or less, then you must make a tactics check (opposed Intelligence roll) to move beween zones.

ISSUE: High speed move, engage (using half movement? maybe 10 ft or 15 ft of movement is better), and then attack.
